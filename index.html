The Perfect First Cast

There's no easier — or cheaper — way to introduce your kid to fishing. No boat, no charter fees, no crazy gear list. Just a rod, a Miami canal, and a guide who makes it fun from the very first cast. It's the ideal way to find out if your child loves the water without spending a fortune to do it.

It's calm, screen-free, and rewarding in a way a controller never will be — and unlike most summer activities, it's something they can carry and enjoy for the rest of their lives. Hook them young, and you've given them a hobby that lasts forever.

Safety First, Always

How We Keep Kids Safe

We fish near open water in a subtropical environment. We're upfront about the risks and manage them carefully — and a signed waiver is required before every trip.

⚡

Weather Permitting

Summer rain is normal and we fish right through it — the bite is often best in a light shower. We only stop for lightning, moving to safe cover immediately and resuming when it clears.

🐊

Wildlife Aware

Miami canals can hold alligators, crocodiles, and snakes. Kids stay back from the water's edge at all times, and Xavi scouts and positions every spot for safety.

🌊

Water Smart

Strictly land-based — no boats. Kids fish from stable banks within sight at all times. Life jackets available and required for non-swimmers near water.

🥵

Heat Managed

Water, shade breaks, hats & sunscreen built into every session. We watch every child for heat and hydration in the Miami sun.

🎣

Hook Handling

Barbless or pinched-barb options for beginners, hands-on coaching on safe casting and fish handling, and a first-aid kit on hand.

Good to Know

FAQ & Waiver

The honest answers parents ask before booking.

What if it rains?
Summer rain in Miami is almost daily and we fish right through it — the bite is often best in a passing shower. We only stop for lightning. If lightning is in the area we move to safe cover and resume once it clears. If a session is cut short by severe weather, we'll reschedule or credit the time.
Are there really alligators and crocodiles?
Yes — Miami's canals can hold American alligators, and brackish coastal systems can hold crocodiles, plus snakes and other wildlife. This is exactly why we're 100% land-based, keep kids back from the water's edge, scout every spot in advance, and require a signed waiver. Respecting the wildlife is part of what the kids learn.
How qualified is Xavi?
Honest answer: I'm an avid fisherman who's spent the past years learning from some of the best local captains and guides, and thousands of hours chasing fish — but I'm not a professional captain or charter guide. I'm just a guy who genuinely loves to fish, loves meeting new people, and wants to teach the younger generation the craft I've enjoyed so much. It started with my dad and my brother, fishing our boats from the Keys to the Bahamas and everywhere in between. Now I want to pass that on. What your kid gets is a patient, safety-first mentor who knows these waters and is excited to share them.
Do you always catch a fish?
Simple answer: no. That's why it's called fishing, not catching! There's never a guarantee on any given day — the fish don't read the schedule. What we do guarantee is getting your kid outside, on the water, learning, and having a great time while we work hard to put them on a fish. Come with the right expectations: it's about the experience and the skills, and the catch is the bonus. Some days they're slamming, some days they're shy — that's nature, and it's part of what makes a real catch feel so good.
What ages do you take? Do kids need a fishing license?
We take kids ages 10 and up. Children under 13 must be accompanied by an adult for the session (a parent or guardian stays with the group). Kids 13 and older can be dropped off. In Florida, children under 16 do not need a fishing license, so sign-up is simple.
What's included? What should my kid bring?
We provide all rods, reels, tackle, and instruction. You bring: a hat, sunscreen, closed-toe shoes, a refillable water bottle, and a sense of adventure. Live bait is purchased by you at the meetup (Kendall Bait & Tackle, ~$15–20/dozen), or skip it and fish artificial lures.
How do I pay?
We keep it simple — payment is by Zelle to (305) 409-3399 once your date is confirmed. No app fees, no hassle. Live bait is purchased separately by you at the meetup (Kendall Bait & Tackle, typically $15–20/dozen), or skip it and fish artificial lures.
Where do we meet?
Trips start by meeting at Kendall Bait & Tackle in Miami-Dade, where we grab bait and gear before heading to the day's spot. Exact meeting details are confirmed when you book.
Can you go to the Keys or saltwater spots?
Absolutely. Beyond our local canals we can arrange excursions to the Keys and other areas, and fish saltwater from bridges and beaches — gas and travel are billed extra. Tell us what species your kid dreams of catching and we'll build the trip.
