Participation Agreement, Assumption of Risk & Liability Release

Youth Land-Based Fishing Program · Miami-Dade County, Florida. This agreement must be read and signed by the parent or legal guardian of each participating minor before the start of any session.

⚠️ Please read this entire agreement carefully. It affects your legal rights, including giving up the right to sue. By signing at the bottom, you confirm you understand and accept it on behalf of yourself and your child.

1.Nature of the Activity

The Program ("Fish with Xavi") is a land-based (shoreline / bank) recreational fishing activity conducted at public parks, canals, lakes, bridges, beaches, and waterways in Miami-Dade County, Florida, and may include arranged excursions to other areas such as the Florida Keys. Activities take place outdoors, on uneven natural terrain, adjacent to open water, in a subtropical climate. No boats or watercraft are used.

2.Assumption of Specific Risks

I understand that outdoor shoreline fishing involves inherent and unavoidable risks that cannot be eliminated regardless of the care taken. I knowingly and voluntarily accept ALL risks, known and unknown, including but not limited to:

  • Wildlife encounters — including alligators, crocodiles, snakes (including venomous species), snapping turtles, iguanas, fire ants, bees, wasps, spiders, and other animals or insects that may bite, sting, or attack.
  • Open water hazards — slipping, falling into, or entering canals, lakes, or waterways; deep water; underwater hazards; and risk of drowning.
  • Fishing equipment — injury from fish hooks, sharp tackle, knives, lures, fishing line, and rods, including embedded hooks and lacerations.
  • Fish — cuts, punctures, fin spines, teeth, and bites from caught fish.
  • Weather & environment — lightning, thunderstorms, sudden weather changes, extreme heat, humidity, dehydration, heat exhaustion, heat stroke, sunburn, and sun exposure.
  • Terrain — slips, trips, and falls on wet, muddy, rocky, sloped, or uneven banks; submerged or hidden obstacles; and vegetation.
  • Environmental exposure — contaminated or bacteria-bearing water, plants (poison ivy, etc.), and allergens.
  • General risks — the conduct of other participants or members of the public, transportation to and between fishing locations, and the possibility of serious bodily injury, permanent disability, or death.

3.Supervision Acknowledgment

I understand the Program provides reasonable supervision but cannot guarantee constant one-on-one supervision of any child, and that conditions near open water require my judgment about my child's readiness for this activity. I confirm my child is able to follow safety instructions and is suited to this activity.

4.Health, Swimming Ability & Medical Authorization

I certify that my child is in good health and able to participate. In the event of injury, accident, or medical emergency, I authorize the Program operator to seek and obtain emergency medical care for my child, and I accept financial responsibility for any such care. I have disclosed all relevant allergies and medical conditions in the form below.

5.Safety Rules (Parent & Child Agree To)

  • Child will stay back from the water's edge as instructed and will never enter the water.
  • Child will follow all hook-handling, casting, and fish-handling instructions given by the guide.
  • Child will remain within the designated fishing area and within sight of the guide.
  • Sessions may be paused, relocated, shortened, or canceled at the operator's sole discretion for lightning, weather, wildlife presence, or any safety concern. The Program fishes through ordinary rain and stops only for lightning, moving to safe cover until it passes.

6.Release of Liability & Covenant Not to Sue

To the fullest extent permitted by Florida law, I, on behalf of myself, my child, and our heirs, executors, and assigns, hereby RELEASE, WAIVE, DISCHARGE, and COVENANT NOT TO SUE Fish with Xavi, its owner(s), guide(s), employees, agents, and volunteers (collectively, the "Released Parties") from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions, or causes of action for any injury, death, illness, or property damage arising out of or related to participation in the Program, including any claim alleging the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties. I understand I am giving up substantial legal rights, including the right to sue.

7.Indemnification

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any loss, liability, claim, or cost (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from my child's participation in the Program or my breach of this Agreement.

8.Severability & Governing Law

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, and venue for any dispute shall lie in Miami-Dade County, Florida.

I have read this agreement, fully understand its terms, understand that I am giving up substantial rights by signing it, and sign it freely and voluntarily without any inducement.
